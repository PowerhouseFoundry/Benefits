Preparation

What you may need

Applications differ, but these are common pieces of information to have ready.

ID

Identity

Name, date of birth, address and proof of identity.

NI

National Insurance

Your National Insurance number, if you have one.

£

Money

Income, payslips, savings, pensions and other benefits.

⌂

Housing

Tenancy agreement, rent amount, landlord details and service charges.

+

Health

Fit notes, professional reports and examples of how your condition affects daily life.

▣

Bank details

Account name, sort code and account number for real claims. Use fictional details in this simulator.

✉

Contact details

Phone, email and any communication adjustments you need.

?

Support

Details of an appointee, advocate or trusted person if someone helps you manage your claim.

Important limitation

The calculators give an indicative training estimate only. Real entitlement can depend on immigration status, exact education rules, earnings periods, housing rules, deductions, benefit caps, previous claims, transitional protection and evidence.

For a real claim, use GOV.UK or an approved benefits calculator/adviser.
