Rules and changes · reviewed 16 November 2026

Work, benefits and what may change

Current rules are different from government announcements and independent recommendations.

CURRENT RULES

Work Capability Assessment

The DWP makes the formal decision. It can decide that a claimant is fit for work, has limited capability for work (LCW), or has limited capability for work and work-related activity (LCWRA). There is also a severe, lifelong health condition outcome.

A person with LCW or LCWRA can still choose to work if they are able. The decision affects Universal Credit responsibilities and possible extra amounts.

Official WCA guidance ↗
CURRENT RULES

Age and education matter

PIP can usually be claimed from age 16, if its conditions are met. Universal Credit normally starts from age 18, with specific exceptions for some 16–17-year-olds. Full-time education often restricts Universal Credit unless an exception applies.

For learners in full-time non-advanced education, seek individual advice rather than assuming a disability or fit note automatically permits a claim.

Universal Credit eligibility ↗
ANNOUNCED · NOT FOR EVERY LEARNER

Youth Jobs Guarantee

The government announced a nationwide expansion on 14 November 2026. Eligible 18–24-year-olds receiving Universal Credit and looking for work can be offered a fully-funded six-month job under the programme.

It does not automatically apply to every 16–19-year-old. Ask an adviser whether you meet the current scheme criteria.

Government announcement ↗
REVIEW PROPOSALS · NOT LAW

The Milburn Review

As at 16 November 2026, the final review is expected on 17 November. Discussion includes employment support, training, apprenticeships, incentives to hire young people and potential changes to benefit expectations.

Suggested changes, including possible consequences for refusing suitable support, must not be treated as existing law or applied to an individual claim without checking what has actually been adopted.

Official research information ↗
WORK SUPPORT

Jobs and reasonable adjustments

Some people can work with visual instructions, flexible breaks, accessible communication, a job coach or equipment. Access to Work may help eligible people with disability-related barriers to paid employment.

Important limitation

The calculators give an indicative training estimate only. Real entitlement can depend on immigration status, exact education rules, earnings periods, housing rules, deductions, benefit caps, previous claims, transitional protection and evidence.

For a real claim, use GOV.UK or an approved benefits calculator/adviser.
